Demystifying the technology that shapes our world. From the invisible infrastructure of cloud computing to the cybersecurity practices protecting your data, our technology articles explain how modern tech works, and why it matters, in language that doesn't require a computer science degree.

Whether you're a complete newcomer trying to understand what cloud computing actually means, a professional trying to get up to speed on cybersecurity basics, or simply someone curious about where technology is heading next, you'll find articles here that treat you as an intelligent adult without burying you in jargon. Every piece is written to be useful long after the news cycle has moved on. Good foundational knowledge doesn't expire.
