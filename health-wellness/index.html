Health & Wellness

Evidence-informed, plainly written health content covering everyday habits, sleep science, nutrition fundamentals, and more. We explain what the research actually says, without overpromising, without fear-mongering, and without forgetting that you should consult your doctor.

Medical Disclaimer: Articles in this category are for general educational purposes only. They do not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making changes to your health regimen. Read our full disclaimer.

Health writing on the internet tends toward extremes: either vague, generic advice ("drink more water, sleep more, stress less") or alarmist claims about superfoods and miracle supplements. InfoNest's approach is different. We dig into what peer-reviewed research actually says about everyday health decisions, how much sleep architecture matters, which nutritional frameworks have the most evidence behind them, which daily habits have genuine longitudinal support, and we present that research honestly, including the caveats. Our goal isn't to replace your doctor. It's to make you a more informed patient and a more thoughtful steward of your own wellbeing.
