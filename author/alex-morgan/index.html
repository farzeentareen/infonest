About Alex

Alex Morgan is the lead editor and primary writer at InfoNest. With a background that spans software development, science communication, and personal finance writing, Alex has spent the better part of a decade making complicated ideas accessible to general audiences.

The through-line in Alex's work has always been a suspicion of oversimplification. Good knowledge writing, as Alex sees it, doesn't talk down to the reader or drown them in jargon, it finds the middle path: precise enough to be true, clear enough to be useful. That philosophy shapes every article on InfoNest.

Before launching InfoNest, Alex wrote for several independent publications covering emerging technology and behavioral economics. Along the way, the same question kept coming up from readers: "Where can I find a single place that covers all of this?" InfoNest is the answer to that question, a curated hub where someone curious about both machine learning and personal finance, or both sleep science and career strategy, can find substantive coverage without bouncing between a dozen sites.

Alex holds a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and a postgraduate qualification in Science Communication. Articles in health and finance categories are reviewed against current clinical and financial guidance and include appropriate professional consultation disclaimers.

When not writing, Alex reads obsessively across disciplines, a habit that tends to produce the most interesting cross-category articles on InfoNest. Found a factual error in an article? Alex wants to know. Email hello@infonest.page.
