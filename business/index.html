Business

Clear, practical business content for people building or running something, from understanding the business model frameworks that underpin every successful company to guerrilla marketing tactics that work on a shoestring budget.

Starting and running a business is harder than most content about entrepreneurship suggests. InfoNest's business articles don't romanticize the process, they explain it honestly: what it actually takes to start a legitimate small business (including the legal and financial groundwork that gets skipped in YouTube tutorials), how different business models work and which fit which kinds of ventures, and how small businesses can market themselves effectively without a large budget. Our business content is built for people who are serious about what they're building and want information they can actually use, not inspiration-porn or hustle-culture rhetoric.
