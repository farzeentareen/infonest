Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

InfoNest ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website located at https://infonest.page. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, your rights in relation to that data, and how we handle advertising and cookies. By using this website, you accept the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information You Provide Voluntarily

When you contact us via the contact form on our Contact page, you provide your name, email address, and message content. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not add you to any mailing list without your explicit consent, and we do not share this information with third parties for marketing purposes.

b) Newsletter Subscriptions

If you choose to subscribe to our newsletter, your email address is collected and stored by our email service provider (Buttondown). Please review Buttondown's privacy policy at buttondown.email/privacy for details on how that service processes your data. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link included in every email we send.

c) Automatically Collected Data

Like most websites, InfoNest automatically collects certain technical information when you visit, including your IP address (truncated/anonymized where possible), browser type and version, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, and time spent on those pages. This data is collected via web analytics tools to help us understand how readers use the site and improve our content.

2. Cookies

We use cookies, small text files stored on your device, for several purposes:

  • Functional cookies: We store your theme preference (light/dark mode) in your browser's local storage. This is not transmitted to any server and does not identify you personally.
  • Analytics cookies: If we use a web analytics service, it may set cookies to distinguish unique visitors and track session activity. This data is aggregated and anonymized.
  • Advertising cookies: Google AdSense sets cookies to serve advertisements on our site. These cookies are used by Google to show you relevant ads based on your prior visits to websites that use Google's advertising services, including this one. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to InfoNest and/or other sites on the internet.

3. Google AdSense and Advertising

InfoNest participates in the Google AdSense program. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site. Google's use of the DoubleClick cookie enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of the use of the DoubleClick cookie for interest-based advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for interest-based advertising by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page or the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page.

Advertisements served by Google AdSense are clearly distinguishable from editorial content on this website. The ads served are managed by Google and are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website may include links to or services provided by third parties. These include:

  • Google AdSense, advertising (see Section 3)
  • Buttondown, email newsletter
  • Formspree, contact form processing

These third-party services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of any external site or service.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to messages and inquiries submitted via our contact form
  • Send newsletters to subscribers who have opted in
  • Understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent or abusive activity
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to any third party.

6. Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and for a reasonable period thereafter. Newsletter subscriber data is retained until you unsubscribe. Automatically collected analytics data is generally retained in aggregated, anonymized form.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access data we hold about you, the right to request correction or deletion of that data, and the right to object to certain types of processing. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@infonest.page.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

8. Children's Privacy

InfoNest is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

9. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect any personal data we hold. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted to or from our site.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page from time to time to stay informed about how we protect your information.
